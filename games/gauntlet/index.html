How to survive

Move: Each arrow, WASD key, direction button, or swipe moves one tile. Attack: Walk into a monster to hit it, or press Space / Attack to strike the way you face.

Escape: Find the key in the upper-right, then reach the exit in the lower-right. Gold adds points; potions restore health. You can dodge monsters instead of defeating them.

Monsters act every other move or attack. Keep moving, and press P to pause. Leaving the window pauses the run.
